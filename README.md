> **Arcline is a journaling and showcase tool designed for creators who want to document their journey.** 
> It's **not** a productivity tracker. Builders come when they have something to log—no daily obligations, no arbitrary streaks.

<br />

## ⚡ Core Philosophy

We celebrate the **journey**, not just the destination. Every day of building requires proof of work. Document the details. Setbacks are not failures — they are valuable data points.

---

## 🧱 The Log System

Builders create **projects**, then log **entries** as they build, whenever they have something meaningful to log. 

| Type | Name | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 🏆 | **WIN** | A victory, big or small. |
| 🩹 | **SETBACK** | A roadblock or failure. Struggling is data. |
| 🏁 | **MILESTONE** | A major checkpoint reached. |
| 🧠 | **REALIZATION** | A key learning, insight, or pivot. |

---

## 🎨 Design Language

Arcline embraces a **brutalist-minimal** aesthetic.

- **Theme**: Dark, monospace-heavy
- **Background:** `#080808`
- **Accent:** `#E8572A` (Safety Orange)
- **Shapes:** Sharp edges, strictly no rounded corners on cards.
