## ⚡ What is Arcline?

Arcline is a "proof-of-work for builders" platform. 

It is a journaling and showcase tool — **NOT** a productivity tracker. Builders create **projects**, then log **entries** as they build, whenever they have something meaningful to log, without any daily obligation.

### Our Core Philosophy
We celebrate the _journey_, not just the destination. Setbacks are not failures — they are valuable data points.

## 🧱 The Log System

Builders can log four main types of entries:
* 🏆 **WIN:** A victory, big or small.
* 🩹 **SETBACK:** A roadblock or failure. Struggling is data.
* 🏁 **MILESTONE:** A major checkpoint reached.
* 🧠 **REALIZATION:** A key learning, insight, or pivot.

## 🎨 Design Language

Arcline embraces a **Dark, monospace-heavy, brutalist-minimal** aesthetic.
* **Background:** `#080808`
* **Accent:** `#E8572A` (Safety Orange)
* **Shapes:** Sharp edges, no rounded corners on cards.
